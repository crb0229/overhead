Reading the sky

  • Steady dot sliding across the sky: a satellite. A blinking one is an airplane.
  • A line of dots, evenly spaced: Starlink, most common in the first days after a launch.
  • Best window: the first hour after dark and the last before dawn, when the sky is dark but the satellite is still catching sunlight.
  • Fades out mid-sky: it just entered Earth's shadow. That is normal.
